# Real Estate Service Management System (RESMS)

## Slide 1: Project Overview

- A web application for managing real estate sales and rentals.
- Keeps properties, customers, appointments, and deals in one place.
- Designed for administrators and real estate agents.

## Slide 2: Problem and Objectives

- Separate paper or spreadsheet records make information difficult to track.
- RESMS organizes records and makes them easier to search and update.
- Helps staff follow each deal from customer inquiry to payment recording.

## Slide 3: User Roles

- **Admin:** Manages all records, customer information, and user accounts.
- **Agent:** Views business records and manages their assigned work.
- Access permissions protect restricted pages and actions.

## Slide 4: Main Features

- **Properties and customers:** Store property details, photos, and customer contacts.
- **Inquiries and appointments:** Track customer interest and schedule viewings.
- **Transactions and contracts:** Manage sale or rental deals and linked agreements.
- **Payments:** Manually record payments and track Paid or Pending status.

## Slide 5: Example Workflow

1. Add a property and customer.
2. Record an inquiry and assign an agent.
3. Schedule a property viewing.
4. Create a sale or rental transaction from the inquiry.
5. Add a contract and record payments.
6. Complete the transaction to mark the property Sold or Rented.

## Slide 6: Dashboard and Reports

- Dashboard shows key totals, upcoming appointments, and a property-status chart.
- Reports display property, transaction, and payment records.
- Search and filters help staff find relevant information quickly.

## Slide 7: Technologies Used

- **Python 3.12 and Django 5:** Application logic and page rendering.
- **PostgreSQL:** Database storage.
- **Bootstrap 5:** Page layout and styling.
- **Chart.js:** Dashboard chart.

## Slide 8: Benefits and Future Improvements

- Centralizes records and makes daily work easier to organize.
- Automatically updates property status when a deal is completed.
- Protects linked business history from accidental deletion.
- **Future ideas:** Appointment reminders, downloadable reports, and online payments.
